ABOUT THE GAME

The Question Game is a text-based horror experience. It asks you questions. You answer them. Then it keeps asking.

It uses a whole bag of tricks to make it look like it is hacking your computer - fake errors, fake terminals, things that seem to read themselves. It is all theatre, built for one purpose: to convince one person that their machine had turned on them.

WARNING: do not play if you have a heart condition, or a computer you are afraid of losing.

THE STORY

I originally made this game to prank my friend, Hazel.

I wanted to convince her that her machine had turned on her. It worked. She screamed. The prank was a success.

Now I am still working on this game. What started as a prank gave me the confidence to make more games - and there are more to come.
