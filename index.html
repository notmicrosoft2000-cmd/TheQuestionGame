> ADDRESSING CONCERNS

Because the game is meant to unsettle you, here is exactly what it does — and does not — do.

IS THIS A VIRUS?
No. The full source is public on GitHub for anyone to read. The game is a single program that runs only while you play it. It does not install anything, modify other software, or register itself to start with your system.
DOES IT USE MY CAMERA OR MICROPHONE?
The classic edition never activates a camera or microphone on either platform. The Remastered edition can briefly request your camera only as an in-fiction scare — deny the permission and the scare is skipped, and the game keeps running. Neither edition records audio, and nothing is ever saved from the camera.
DOES IT SEND MY ANSWERS ANYWHERE?
The classic edition makes no network requests at all — everything you type stays in a local save file on your machine. The Remastered edition can call an AI service to write your questions when a network is available (it works offline with built-in questions otherwise), but the answers you choose are sent only in a short line of generated-fiction context, are never stored by the developer, and remain yours. Either way, playable fully disconnected.
IT SOUNDS SKETCHY. WHAT DOES IT ACTUALLY DO TO MY SYSTEM?
A short list of scripted effects: it plays audio, briefly moves or shakes its own window, can change your desktop wallpaper, and may show a full-screen flash. These are timed events inside the fiction. It cannot lock you out, delete files, or control anything outside its own window and the two effects above. Effects are per-session and do not persist after you quit.
IS IT SAFE TO PLAY?
It is designed to startle you: sudden sounds, flashes, and screen changes. Do not play if you have photosensitive epilepsy or are prone to panic reactions, and do not play while driving, working, or in a context where being startled is a hazard. There is no real danger — but you should decide for yourself, with the lights on first.
HOW DO I UNINSTALL IT?
Windows: delete the folder you unzipped — nothing else was installed. macOS: delete The Question Game.app from Applications, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/TheQuestionGame if you also want your local answers gone. That is the entire uninstall process.
WHERE DO I REPORT A BUG OR A PROBLEM?
Open an issue on the GitHub repository (linked in THE DEV section). Bug reports are read and answered. If the game did something you did not expect on your system, that is exactly what we want to hear about.

⚠ STROBE / PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING

THE QUESTION GAME CONTAINS FLASHING LIGHTS, RAPID SCREEN CHANGES AND A SUDDEN FULL-SCREEN FLASH AS PART OF ITS SCARES. THIS SITE PREVIEW ALSO FLASHES.

If you or anyone playing has photosensitive epilepsy, or has ever had a seizure triggered by screens or flashing lights, do not play. A small percentage of people may experience symptoms even without a prior history.

Play in a well-lit room, at a safe distance from the screen, and stop immediately if you feel any discomfort.

INSTALL GUIDE

  1. Unzip the download. Open the ZIP in File Explorer and click Extract All (or right-click the ZIP → Extract All).
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click TheQuestionGame.exe (Classic) or TheQuestionGameRemastered.exe (Remastered).
  3. Windows SmartScreen: Windows will likely show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is unsigned. Click More info → Run anyway.
  4. Still blocked? Right-click the .exe → Properties → tick Unblock → OK, then run it again.
  5. That's it. No installation. To uninstall, delete the folder you unzipped.
